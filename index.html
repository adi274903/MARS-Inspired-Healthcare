MARS – Healthcare

An assistant that helps researchers and developers work out what to evaluate, and what to take into account, when building healthcare AI.

About MARS

MARS is the Model-Agnostic Rigorous Sandbox, an open-source testing and evaluation environment for AI system measurement from the CMU–NIST AI Measurement Science & Engineering Cooperative Research Center (AIMSEC). It splits an evaluation into three modular parts: a Corpus of test cases, a Workflow that runs the system under test, and an Umpire that assesses the outputs. This project brings that measurement-first approach to healthcare AI.
